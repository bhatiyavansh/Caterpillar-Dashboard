## Know the machine and the plan
Operate only machines you are trained and authorised for. Know the day's task, the work area limits, where people and other machines will be, and the site's traffic rules.

## Stay aware
Look before every movement, keep mirrors and cameras clean, and keep checking for people. Expect people and vehicles to appear where you do not expect them.

## Respect warnings
Warnings, fault codes and safety alerts exist to stop an incident before it happens. Stop, understand the warning and follow the site protocol before continuing. Never disable or bypass a safety system.

## Work within limits
Do not overload the attachment, work on slopes steeper than the site plan allows, or work beyond what you can see. When conditions change, slow down or stop and ask.

## Report
Report faults, near misses and unsafe conditions straight away. Reports are how the site learns and fixes problems before someone is hurt.
