## Choose a safe place
Park on firm, level ground away from excavation edges, traffic routes and overhead power lines. If parking on a slope cannot be avoided, turn the machine so it cannot roll and follow the site rule for blocking the tracks or wheels.

## Lower everything
Lower the bucket, blade or other attachment flat to the ground. This removes stored energy from the hydraulics and stops the attachment dropping later.

## Shut down
Put the controls in neutral, engage the hydraulic lockout and parking brake, and let the engine idle briefly if the machine's manual asks for a cool-down. Then switch off the engine and remove the key.

## Leaving the cab
Unfasten the seatbelt only after the engine is off. Climb down facing the machine, using the steps and handholds. Report any fault or warning seen during the shift before leaving.
