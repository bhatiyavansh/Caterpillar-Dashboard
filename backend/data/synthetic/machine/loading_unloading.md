## Loading a truck
Position the truck so the excavator slews the shortest distance and never swings the bucket over the truck's cab. The truck driver stays in the cab or in an agreed safe place, never beside the truck while it is being loaded. Place material evenly and avoid dropping it from height.

## Spillage and falling material
Material can fall from a moving bucket. Nobody should stand under or near a load being lifted or swung. Keep the bucket as low as practical while slewing.

## Loading and unloading a machine for transport
Use a level, firm area and ramps designed for the machine. Line the machine up with the ramps before driving up, travel slowly and do not steer on the ramps. Once on the trailer, lower the attachment, shut down, and secure the machine as the transport procedure requires.

## Communication
The machine operator and truck drivers agree on signals before loading begins. When the queue at a loader backs up, drivers wait in the marked area rather than approaching the working machine.
