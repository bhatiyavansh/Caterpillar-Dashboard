## Before moving
Check the route is clear, look in the direction of travel and check mirrors and cameras. Sound the horn before moving off if people may be nearby. Confirm which way the tracks face relative to the cab.

## Travelling
Travel at a speed that lets you stop well within the distance you can see. Keep the attachment low and close to the machine. Go straight up and down slopes rather than across them where possible, and avoid sudden turns on slopes.

## Reversing
Reversing is where most struck-by incidents happen. Look behind before and during reversing, use cameras and mirrors, and use a spotter when the view is blocked. Stop immediately if you lose sight of the spotter.

## Stability
A machine becomes less stable when it carries a load high or far out, works on a side slope, or travels over soft or uneven ground. On this site the tip-over margin gauge shows how close the machine is to its stability limit; its colour bands are described in the Demo site manual (tip-over gauge page).

## When a warning appears
If a safety warning appears while moving, stop, find out why, and follow the site protocol for that warning before continuing. Do not work around an alert.
