## Jerky control inputs
Symptom: the machine lurches and the bucket bounces. Cause: moving controls fully and suddenly. Correction: rest your hand or forearm for support, ease the control in slowly, and hold it steady. Practise starting and stopping each movement as gently as possible.

## Overshooting a slew
Symptom: the upper structure swings past the target and has to come back. Cause: releasing the control too late. Correction: start easing off before the target, let the swing slow down, and approach the last part slowly. Example: to face a truck, start slowing as the bucket passes the truck's front corner.

## Forgetting the direction of travel
Symptom: pushing the travel controls forward and the machine moves backward. Cause: the upper structure is turned relative to the tracks. Correction: check where the drive sprockets are before every travel movement.

## Travelling with the load high
Symptom: the machine rocks or feels light on one side. Cause: the boom is raised or the stick is extended while travelling. Correction: lower the attachment and keep it close to the machine before moving, especially on uneven ground.

## Not looking behind
Symptom: a proximity warning during a reverse or slew. Cause: relying on memory instead of checking. Correction: before every reverse or slew, look over your shoulder and check mirrors and cameras, even if the area was clear a minute ago.
