## Why fatigue matters
A tired operator reacts more slowly, misses hazards and makes more control errors. Fatigue builds over long shifts, repetitive work, heat, poor sleep and night work, and people are poor judges of how tired they are.

## Warning signs
Heavy eyelids, long blinks, yawning, drifting attention, missing a signal or a turn, and not remembering the last few minutes are all signs of fatigue. Microsleeps can happen with the eyes partly open.

## What to do
Stop in a safe place and park the machine properly. Tell the supervisor. Take a break, get fresh air and water, and do not return to operating until alert. Caffeine or turning up the radio only masks fatigue for a short time.

## On this site
The site watches for fatigue using the machine's fatigue score and the cab camera, which can raise a fatigue alert. The thresholds are in the Demo site manual (operator fatigue page) and the steps are in the site fatigue protocol. The alert comes from fixed rules and the camera detector, not from the assistant.
