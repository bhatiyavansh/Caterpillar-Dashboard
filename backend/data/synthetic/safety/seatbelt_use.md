## Why the seatbelt matters
Earthmoving machines are fitted with a rollover or tip-over protective structure. That structure only protects an operator who stays inside it. In a rollover or sudden stop, an unbelted operator can be thrown out of the seat or out of the cab and crushed by the machine. The seatbelt keeps the operator inside the protected zone.

## When to wear it
Fasten the seatbelt before starting the engine and keep it fastened whenever the machine is running, including short moves and idling. Unfasten it only after the machine is parked, the attachment is lowered and the engine is off.

## What the cab does on this site
On this site the cab display escalates a seatbelt warning while the engine is running and the belt is unfastened. The escalation steps and timings are described in the Demo site manual (seatbelt escalation page) and the steps to follow are in the site seatbelt protocol. The warning is raised by fixed rules on the machine, not by the assistant.

## Habits that help
Adjust the seat first, then fasten the belt, so it fits properly. Replace a belt that is frayed, cut or does not latch; report it rather than working without one. If leaving the seat, shut down properly first instead of leaving the engine running.
