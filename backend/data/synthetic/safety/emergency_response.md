## First, make it safe
In any emergency, stop the machine's movement first. Lower the attachment to the ground if it is safe to do so, and use the emergency stop if the machine will not respond normally. Do not leave the cab into a hazard such as a fallen power line or a collapsing edge.

## Raise the alarm
Use the radio or the site's emergency procedure to report what happened, where, and whether anyone is hurt. Give clear, short information. Follow the site's emergency plan for who to call and where to meet.

## Help only if safe
Help an injured person only if you can do so without becoming a second casualty. Do not move a seriously injured person unless they are in immediate danger. Trained first aiders and emergency services take over as soon as they arrive.

## Afterwards
Leave the scene as it is where possible so it can be investigated. Report the incident through the site's incident reporting protocol so the cause can be found and fixed. Near misses should be reported too: they are the warning before the next incident.
