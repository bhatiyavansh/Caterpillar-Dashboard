## Ground conditions change
Rain softens ground, turns haul roads slippery and can undermine the edges of excavations and embankments. A slope that was safe in the morning can be unsafe after rain. Travel slower, keep further from edges and avoid side slopes where possible.

## Visibility
Rain, fog and dust reduce how far the operator can see and how well people on foot can be seen. Use lights, slow down, keep windows, mirrors and cameras clean, and increase the distance kept from people and other machines.

## Plan the work around the weather
Some tasks are much riskier in the wet, such as working near trench edges or on slopes. Supervisors may re-sequence tasks so weather-sensitive work waits. On this site the task plan can be reordered for rain, and any reorder is only applied after a person confirms it.

## Lightning and extreme conditions
Stop work and follow the site's weather procedure when lightning is near or conditions make it impossible to see hazards. A machine is not a safe place to wait out lightning unless the site procedure says so.
