## Be seen
People on foot should wear high-visibility clothing and stay where operators expect them: marked walkways and agreed areas. Never walk behind a working machine or through its swing area.

## Make contact before approaching
Before approaching a machine, get the operator's attention and wait for a clear signal that they have seen you and stopped. Approach from where the operator can see you. Eye contact is the simplest confirmation.

## Stay out of the line of fire
Do not stand under a raised load or bucket, next to a truck being loaded, or between a machine and a fixed object. Spillage and swinging loads travel further than expected.

## Spotters
A spotter guides a machine where the operator cannot see. The operator and spotter agree on signals before starting, the spotter stays in the operator's view, and the operator stops immediately if they lose sight of the spotter.
