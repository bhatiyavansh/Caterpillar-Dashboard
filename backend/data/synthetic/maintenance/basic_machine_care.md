## Daily care
Keep the machine clean enough to see leaks and damage, especially around the engine, hydraulics and undercarriage. Clear mud and debris from the tracks and around moving parts. Keep the cab windows, mirrors and cameras clean.

## Lubrication
Grease the pins and bushings the machine's manual lists, at the intervals it gives. Wipe grease fittings before greasing so dirt is not pushed in. Pins that squeak or look dry need attention.

## Fluids and filters
Check fluid levels as part of the pre-start inspection and use only the fluids the manual specifies. Change filters at the intervals in the manual or when the machine's service indicators ask for it. A level that keeps dropping points to a leak.

## Records and forecasts
Record checks, top-ups and faults so trends are visible. On this site the maintenance forecast estimates hours until service for each component from the machine's data, and a work order is only raised after a person confirms it.

## Know when to stop
Stop and report instead of carrying on when a warning lamp or fault code appears, a component is damaged, or the machine behaves differently from normal.
