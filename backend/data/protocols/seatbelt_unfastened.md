---
id: PRT-SEATBELT
title: Seatbelt unfastened while operating
applies_to_events: [seatbelt_unfastened]
severity: high
roles: [operator, supervisor]
steps:
  - "Bring the machine to a controlled stop and lower the attachment to the ground."
  - "Fasten the seatbelt before any further travel or implement movement."
  - "If the belt or buckle is damaged, keep the machine parked and report it to the supervisor for repair."
  - "Resume work only after the cab shows the seatbelt as fastened."
escalation:
  - "Level 3 (travel locked message) not cleared within 60 seconds: supervisor contacts the operator by radio."
  - "Repeated violations in one shift are logged for a coaching conversation, not a penalty."
source: "Demo site SOP (CAT Copilot hackathon)"
regulation:
  citation: "29 CFR 1926.602(a)(2)(i)"
  quote: "Seat belts shall be provided on all equipment covered by this section and shall meet the requirements of the Society of Automotive Engineers, J386-1969, Seat Belts for Construction Equipment."
---
