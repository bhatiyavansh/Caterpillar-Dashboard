---
id: PRT-FATIGUE
title: Operator fatigue detected
applies_to_events: [fatigue_alert]
severity: high
roles: [operator, supervisor]
steps:
  - "Finish the current movement safely and park the machine with the attachment lowered."
  - "Take a rest break away from the cab of at least 15 minutes."
  - "Drink water and tell the supervisor you have taken a fatigue break."
  - "Resume only when you feel alert; if fatigue returns, ask the supervisor to swap operators."
escalation:
  - "Two fatigue alerts in one shift: the supervisor assigns a relief operator."
source: "Demo site SOP (CAT Copilot hackathon)"
---
